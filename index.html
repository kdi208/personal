What I believe

Rome kept a million people quiet with bread and circuses. We are building the new bread and circuses in our own labs.

Feeds that choose for us, assistants that think for us, and a future where AI does the work and a monthly check covers the rest. I refuse it. A safety net is fine. A hammock is not.

Not all struggle is sacred. Disease, hunger and needless suffering ennoble no one, and technology should end them without apology. But the struggle to learn, to build, to choose, to master something hard is what makes us who we are. Erase the first and you free people. Erase the second and you empty them.

Good AI pushes us into harder struggles. Bad AI takes ours away.

Come on the show

If you build technology and you've thought hard about what it means for people, I want to talk to you. You don't need to be famous. I'm looking for the most thoughtful founder, not the most famous one.

The conversation runs 60 to 90 minutes, filmed, in person or remote. You get three or four questions ahead of time and nothing else to prepare.

Write to me. Know someone who belongs on the show? Tell me who.

About

I'm Dino Ilievski. I've spent my career where technology meets the people who live with it: as a Congressional fellow working on tax policy, as a corporate lawyer, and as the founder of a medical Q&A software company. I've invested in startups. Princeps is the question I kept asking in all of those rooms.
