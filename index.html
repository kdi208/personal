Princeps

Princeps is how I back frontier founders: advising, investing, and a podcast about the future they're building. The word is Latin for "the first": the one who goes ahead and shows the way.

I work with a small number of founders, in person, in San Francisco. I take one company per market, so I never advise your competitor.

What we work on

  • Vision and story. We find the future you're building and put it into words you'll use for years: in the pitch, in hiring, on stage.
  • Pitch feedback. A sharp, honest read before the rooms that matter.
  • Your slot is yours. One company per market. No conflicts.
  • The right people. Introductions to hires and specialists I trust.
  • The right investors. Introductions to investors who fit what you're building.

Engagements run twelve months, with set hours each month, always in person. Clients appear in the Princeps portfolio.

Who it's for

Founders in the Bay Area building frontier technology, who have raised and are getting ready for what comes next.
