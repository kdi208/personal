Charismatic
Authority

Definition: A form of authority in which the legitimacy of a leader derives not from position or tradition, but from the personal charisma — the perceived extraordinary qualities — of the leader themselves.

Introduction

As we approach the singularity — arriving, by most serious estimates, around 2045 — the number of things worth working on has collapsed to roughly five.

  • i. Artificial Intelligence
  • ii. Compute
  • iii. Brain-Computer Interface
  • iv. Longevity
  • v. Simulation

Everything else is maintenance. Important maintenance, perhaps — but maintenance nonetheless.

The Standard

The London Times, 1914

Men Wanted for Hazardous Journey

The Offer

If you are working on one of these five things and need human or financial capital, I will meet with you.

If I think you are a future Napoleon — a person whose force of will is so singular that history bends around them — I will fund you.
